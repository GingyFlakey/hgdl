-Rules-

  1. For a level to be submitted to the HGDL, it must be at least tier 10 on the Geometry Dash Demon Ladder.

  2. Each levels difficulty is dependent on GDDL tiers, and no other sources such as the AREDL. However, both AREDL and GDDL information will be provided in the level details.

Gold colored list numbers mean that the thumbnail shown for that level on the list was created by a Gurtwall member. More information about the creator of the thumbnail can be found in the list level's details. Gold thumbnail borders mean that the level is deemed peak by a majority of the level's victors or members with experience from the level (that is a Gurtwall member, of course).
